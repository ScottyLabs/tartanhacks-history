TARTANHACKS is the largest Hackathon in Pittsburgh! Organized by ScottyLabs, it's a 24-hour hackathon where participants from all over the country create innovative projects. Come on over to hack, learn from our workshops and incredible speakers, and meet other hackers.

The 2026 theme was...

MOSAIC

For 24 hours, participants stepped into a world built from fragments,
floating tiles, ideas that refract and snap into place.

Nothing arrived whole; everything began as a piece. Teams gathered scattered bits of vision, skill, and code, assembling them into something greater than any single part. Every insight became a tile and every teammate a new color. As the hours passed, the picture sharpened, a Mosaic taking shape.

Entered with fragments.
Left with something whole.
